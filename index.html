How to use Piano Chord Finder

1. Click the piano keys that make up the chord you want to identify. You can select the notes in any order.

2. The app will identify the chord, its root note and its inversion. The inversion is worked out from the lowest piano key you selected.

3. To remove a note, click the highlighted key again. To start over, click Clear.

Example: Select E3, G3, B♭3 and C4. Because E is the lowest note, the app should identify C7 — 1st inversion.
