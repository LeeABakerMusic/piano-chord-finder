How to use Piano Chord Finder

1. Click the piano keys that make up the chord you want to identify. You can select the notes in any order.

2. The app will identify the chord, its root note and its inversion. The inversion is worked out from the lowest piano key you selected.

3. Use Play Chord to hear all selected notes together, or Play Arpeggio to hear them one at a time from lowest to highest.

4. Key Sound is switched on by default. When it is on, clicking a piano key or playing a MIDI note produces sound through your computer. Untick Key Sound if you want to select notes silently. This does not disable the Play Chord or Play Arpeggio buttons.

5. Press Why? for a simple explanation of how the chord is built.

6. To transpose the chord, choose the new root note from the Transpose to menu, then click the Transpose button. The app will move the chord to the nearest practical position on the displayed piano while keeping the same voicing and inversion.

7. To remove a note, click the highlighted key again. To start over, click Clear.

8. Show Me A Chord: Use this feature when you know the chord you want to see but aren't sure which piano keys to play. Choose the Root (for example C), choose the Chord type (for example Major), then choose the Inversion. Root Position shows the normal chord shape, while 1st, 2nd and other inversions rearrange the same chord notes with a different note at the bottom. Press Show Chord and the correct piano keys will be highlighted. You can then use Play Chord or Play Arpeggio to hear it, and the other chord tools continue to work as normal.

9. MIDI Keyboard: Connect your MIDI controller to your computer, open the app in a browser supporting Web MIDI (such as Chrome or Edge) on the HTTPS website, then press Enable MIDI and allow access if prompted. Choose your MIDI input if necessary. Playing notes highlights the same on-screen keys as clicking them. You can still use the mouse. Hold notes after release is off by default; tick it if you want notes to remain highlighted after releasing them. Press Clear or hold C4, C♯4, D4, D♯4 and E4 together on your MIDI keyboard to clear the chord in Hold mode. Key Sound works with both mouse clicks and MIDI notes, and can be switched off if your keyboard already produces sound.

Example: Select E3, G3, B♭3 and C4. Because E is the lowest note, the app should identify C7 — 1st inversion.
